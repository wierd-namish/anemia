# Participant / Parent Information Leaflet (English)

**Document Identifier:** ETH-SUB-DOC-001  
**Study Title:** Prospective Multi-Center Clinical Validation of an AI Fingernail Assessment System for Pediatric Anemia Screening in Primary Healthcare Settings  
**Principal Investigator:** Dr. A. Mensah-Bonsu, FGCP (Pediatrics)  
**Study Sites:** Princess Marie Louise Children's Hospital, Ga West Municipal Hospital, Central Region Community Health Center  
**Ethics Submission Status:** Prepared for ethics submission (PENDING ETHICS APPROVAL)  

---

### What is the purpose of this study?
Anemia (low blood count or low hemoglobin) is a common and treatable condition in young children. Currently, checking for anemia requires taking a blood sample with a needle. Researchers in Ghana are studying an **investigational research system** that uses a standard smartphone camera to take close-up photographs of children's fingernails to see if computer software can help detect signs of anemia non-invasively.

We are inviting your child to participate in this research study to help us determine how accurately this smartphone tool works compared to standard hospital laboratory blood tests.

---

### Is this smartphone tool a proven medical diagnosis?
**No.** This smartphone tool is an **experimental, investigational research system**. It has **not** been approved as a final diagnostic medical device and cannot replace a laboratory blood test. Your child will still receive standard hospital laboratory blood testing.

---

### What will happen if you agree to participate?
If you decide to let your child join the study:
1. **Fingernail Photography:** A trained study nurse will use a smartphone camera to take 3 to 4 photographs of your child's fingernails. This is completely painless, takes less than one minute, and uses no bright flash.
2. **Standard Blood Test:** Your child will have a routine blood sample collected by a certified laboratory technician as part of their hospital visit (or a small capillary finger-prick).
3. **Medical Care:** The hospital doctor or clinical team will receive your child's laboratory blood test results and provide all standard medical treatment and medicines. The experimental smartphone software will not delay or alter your child's medical care.

---

### Are there any risks?
* **Fingernail Photos:** Taking photos with a mobile phone carries no physical risk or pain.
* **Blood Draw:** Having a blood sample taken may cause slight, brief discomfort or minor bruising at the needle site, which is standard for routine medical care.

---

### Are there any direct benefits?
Your child will receive prompt laboratory blood testing for anemia. If anemia is identified by the laboratory, your child will immediately receive standard nutritional advice, iron supplements, or medical treatment per Ghana Ministry of Health clinical guidelines.

---

### Privacy and Confidentiality
All photographs and study information will be labeled only with a secret study code number (UUID), not your child's name. Photographs will focus strictly on the fingernails; no photos of your child's face will be taken. All information is stored securely and encrypted.

---

### Voluntary Participation & Right to Withdraw
Joining this study is completely voluntary. You may choose not to participate, or you may withdraw your child at any time without giving a reason. Your decision will **not** affect your child's medical care, treatment, or relationship with the hospital staff.
