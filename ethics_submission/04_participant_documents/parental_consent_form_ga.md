* Minu shishi akɛ nɛkɛ fon mfididwuma nɛɛ ji **nikasɛm kɛkɛ**, ni jeee helatsamɛawe lá kwɛmɔ tsakemɔ.

* Minu shishi akɛ abaatwa mibinuatso wao mfoniri ni abagba elá bibioo akɛkwɛ helatsamɛawe.
* Minu shishi akɛ abaaŋɔ mibinuatso sane afee teŋ mli nilee koraa.
* Mikpɛlɛ nɔ kɛ misuɔmɔ fɛɛ akɛ mibinuatso abote nikasɛm nɛɛ mli.
