### Awofoɔ Anaa Ɔhwɛfoɔ Nsiatene:
* Mesi so dua sɛ mafe nhwehwɛmu ho krataa (Version 2.1) no na mate aseɛ, anaa wɔakenkan akyerɛ me wɔ kasa a mete aseɛ mu (Twi / Ga / Borɔfo).
* Menyaa kwan b проверisaa nsɛmmisa, na nhwehwɛmufoɔ no buaa me nsɛmmisa nyinaa yie ma me kɔn dɔeɛ.
* Mete aseɛ sɛ saa fon mfididwuma yi yɛ **nhwehwɛmu foforɔ kɛkɛ**, na ɛnyɛ ayaresabea mogya nhwehwɛmu ankasa ananmu si.
* Mete aseɛ sɛ me ba no ho a ɔde bɛhyɛ mu no yɛ me pɛ mu ade koraa, na metumi agyae berɛ biara a ɛrennsɛe me ba no ayaresa pa a ɔtaa nya no.
* Mete aseɛ sɛ wɔbɛtwa me ba no nsensan mfonini na wɔatwe ne mogya kakra ayɛ nhwehwɛmu wɔ ayaresabea hɔ.
* Mete aseɛ sɛ yɛbɛkora me ba no nsɛm so wɔ kokoam koraa.
* Mepene so koraa sɛ me ba no nyɛ saa nhwehwɛmu yi ho.
