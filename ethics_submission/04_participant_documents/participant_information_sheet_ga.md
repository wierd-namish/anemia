# Nilee Wolo Kɛha Fɔlɔi / Gbɛjianɔtooi (Ga)

**Wolo Nɔmba:** ETH-SUB-DOC-003  
**Nikasɛm Saneyitso:** Nikasɛm Ni Kɔɔ Mfonitwa Heŋmɛi (AI) He Ni Akɛ Kwɛɔ Gbekɛbii Alá Fa He Wɔ Gaana  
**Nikasɛmlatɔ Yitso:** Dr. A. Mensah-Bonsu  
**He Ni Atsuɔ Nitsumɔ Lɛ Yɛ:** Princess Marie Louise Helatsamɛawe, Ga West Helatsamɛawe, kɛ Mfinimfini Mantam Helatsamɛawe  
**Shidaamɔ:** Esa Kɛha GHS-ERC Gbɛjianɔtoo (PENDING ETHICS APPROVAL)  

---

### Mɛni Hewɔ Atsuɔ Nɛkɛ Nikasɛm Nɛɛ?
Lá shwɛmɔ (anemia) ji hela ni baa gbekɛbii nketewa amɛhe waa. Amrɔ nɛɛ, ja akɛ abɛ kpa lá dani anyɛ ale akɛ gbekɛ ko lá eshwɛ aloo dabi. Nitsulɔi kɛ datrɛfoi ni yɔɔ Gaana miisumɔ ni amɛtsɔɔ **fon heŋmɛi mfididwuma (AI)** nɔ amɛtwa gbekɛbii amɛwao mfoniri kɛkwɛ kɛji ebaanyɛ etsɔɔ lá shwɛmɔ mli kɛtsɔ nɔ ko nɔ ni egbako lá.

Wɔmifɔ bo nine ni oha obinuatso afee nɛkɛ nikasɛm nɛɛ mli gbɛfã koni wɔkwɛ kɛji fon nɛɛ baatsu nii pɛpɛɛpɛ tamɔ bɔ ni dɔkita lá kwɛmɔ tsuɔ nii yɛ helatsamɛawe lɛ.

---

### Ani nɛkɛ fon mfididwuma nɛɛ ji nɔ ko ni akpɛlɛ nɔ akɛ tsabaa ni eye emuu?
**Dabi.** Nɛkɛ fon nitsumɔ nɛɛ ji **nikasɛm kɛkɛ**. Etsakeee dɔkita lá kwɛmɔ ni akɛtsaa gbekɛbii lɛ. Obinuatso lɛ baaná lá kwɛmɔ nitsumɔ kɛ dɔkita tsabaa ni he hiaa lɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ.

---

### Mɛni baaba kɛji okpɛlɛ nɔ akɛ obinuatso baabote mli?
1. **Wao mfonitwa:** Nɛɛsi ni akase nii baatsɔ fon heŋmɛi nɔ atwa obinuatso lɛ ewao mfoniri etɛ aloo ejwɛ. Etsɔɔɔ nɔ ko, eyeŋ be koraa, ni la tswaa ko bɛ mli.
2. **Helatsamɛawe Lá Kwɛmɔ:** Dɔkita lá nitsulɔ baagba obinuatso lá bibioo ko tamɔ bɔ ni feeɔ daa yɛ dɔkita we lɛ.
3. **Tsabaa:** Dɔkita lɛ baadamɔ lá nitsumɔ lɛ nɔ aha obinuatso tsofa ni he hiaa lɛ amrɔ nɔŋŋ. Fon mfonitwa nikasɛm lɛ tsiŋ gbekɛ lɛ tsabaa sɛɛ koraa.

---

### Oshidaai ko yɛ mli lo?
* **Wao Mfonitwa:** Fon mfonitwa lɛ bɛ gbeyei aloo nɔdɔmɔ ko koraa.
* **Lá Gbemɔ:** Lá ni agbaa lɛ baanyɛ adɔ gbekɛ lɛ bibioo tamɔ bɔ ni lá gbemɔ feɔ daa lɛ.

---

### Sɛɛnamɔ ni yɔɔ mli
Obinuatso lɛ baaná lá he sanebimɔ pɛpɛɛpɛ oyayaayi. Kɛji ana akɛ elá eshwɛ lɛ, dɔkita lɛ baaha lɛ aduane kɛ tsofa ni woɔ lá oyayaayi tamɔ Gaana Akwahosan Mla tsɔɔ lɛ.

---

### Teŋ Mli Nilee (Confidentiality)
Wɔkɛ obinuatso gbɛi aloo ehiɛ mfoniri woŋ wolo mli. Wao lɛ pɛ mfoniri wɔbaatwa, ni wɔbaaha lɛ nɔmba krɛdɛɛ koni mɔ ko mɔ ko akale akɛ lɛ ji gbekɛ lɛ.

---

### Suɔmɔ Nɔ Jiemɔ Kɛ Gbɛjianɔtoo Shiɛmɔ
Ohe ni obaafee nɛkɛ nikasɛm nɛɛ mli lɛ jɛ o diɛŋtsɛ osuɔmɔ mli. Kɛji osumɔɔɔ lɛ, etsiŋ gbekɛ lɛ tsabaa kɛ helatsamɛawe lɛ suɔmɔ koraa. Obaanyɛ oshi nikasɛm nɛɛ be fɛɛ be ni dɔbii ko bɛ mli.
