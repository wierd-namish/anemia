# Data Retention, Archival, and Deletion Policy

**Document Identifier:** ETH-SUB-DAT-003  
**Date:** 2026-09-30  
**Ethics Submission Status:** Prepared for ethics submission (PENDING ETHICS APPROVAL)

## 1. Retention Schedule

1. **Physical Consent Forms:** Retained in locked, fire-resistant filing cabinets at participating hospital sites for **5 years** post-study completion per GHS-ERC guidelines.
2. **De-Identified Research Database:** Encrypted research dataset retained on secure server storage for **5 years** for regulatory verification, after which data will be securely sanitized using NIST SP 800-88 cryptographic erase standards.
3. **Biological Specimens:** Whole blood samples are consumed during analyzer aspiration or safely autoclaved/disposed of as biohazardous waste within **24 hours**. No long-term biological specimen banking will occur.
4. **Participant Right to Erasure:** If a parent/guardian withdraws consent prior to database lock, all digital image records and laboratory linkages associated with their child's UUID will be deleted within 7 working days.
