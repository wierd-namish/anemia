# Clinical Target Population Specification

## 1. Locked Target Cohort Definition

* **Locked Population:** **Pediatric Population ($\le 5$ Years Old) in Primary Care & Community Clinic Settings**.
* **Geographic Origin:** Sunyani Municipal District, Bono Region, Ghana (West Africa).
* **Demographic & Physiological Profile:**
  * **Age Range:** $0$ to $59$ months (Infants and young children $\le 5$ years).
  * **Biological Sex:** Both male and female pediatric patients represented.
  * **Ethnic / Phototypic Representation:** Predominantly West African descent, representing **Fitzpatrick Skin Phototypes V and VI** (deeply pigmented periungual skin).
  * **Pregnancy Applicability:** **NOT APPLICABLE** (Cohort is exclusively pediatric $\le 5$ years old).
* **Clinical Setting:**
  * Outpatient clinics and primary pediatric triage at Sunyani Municipal Hospital.
  * Venous / capillary blood sampling performed alongside non-invasive nail photography.

---

## 2. Clinical Inclusion and Exclusion Criteria

### A. Inclusion Criteria
1. Children aged $0$ to $5$ years presenting for pediatric care or routine screening.
2. Written informed consent from parents or legal guardians.
3. Successful paired collection of:
   * Laboratory-verified blood hemoglobin measurement ($\text{g/dL}$).
   * Digital fingernail photographs across one or more digits.

### B. Exclusion Criteria & Known Contraindications
1. Children older than $5$ years (until dedicated adult training data is incorporated).
2. Presence of opaque cosmetic coatings (nail polish, artificial pigments, henna) on nail surfaces.
3. Significant trauma, subungual hematoma, or extensive fungal onychomycosis obscuring the nail matrix/bed.
4. Severe acute hemodynamic instability (shock, severe hypothermia with peripheral vasoconstriction).

---

## 3. Generalizability Limitations (Zero Over-Claim Boundary)

> [!WARNING]
> **Regulatory Generalization Warning:**
> The current primary training data is derived exclusively from a pediatric West African population (Fitzpatrick V–VI). The resulting diagnostic model **MUST NOT** be claimed or deployed as a general adult, pregnant, or multi-ethnic diagnostic tool without prior multi-center external validation on adult cohorts and diverse skin phototypes (Fitzpatrick I–IV).
