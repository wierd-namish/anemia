## 2. Institutional Site Coverage & Ethical Governance Summary

1. **No Presumed Coverage:** None of the 3 participating clinical sites is assumed to have ethical clearance prior to formal issuance of the GHS-ERC clearance certificate and institutional endorsement letters.
2. **Standardized Oversight:** All 3 sites operate under the unified Phase 6 Clinical Protocol (v2.1) and Reference Standard SOP (SOP-CLIN-REF-001).
3. **Harmonization Requirement:** Prior to prospective participant enrollment at any site, the monthly 5-sample inter-laboratory split cross-check must demonstrate analyzer comparability (bias $< 0.3\text{ g/dL}$).
