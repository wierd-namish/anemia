# Patient Count Reconciliation Report (552 vs 554)

## 1. Executive Finding
* **Original Mendeley Study Records (Asare et al. 2022):** **552 unique pediatric patients**.
* **Unified Metadata Parsed:** **554 unique `patient_id` strings** across 4,260 images.
* **Discrepancy:** **+2 artificial patient IDs**.

---

## 2. Root Cause Analysis
The 2 extra patient IDs were generated by **spelling typos and inconsistent spacing in the original image filenames** when parsed by regex during repository ingestion:

1. **Typo in 'Anemic' string:**
   * In several files, the string `Anemic` was mistyped as `Anmeic` (inverting 'm' and 'e').
2. **Inconsistent Space Delimiters:**
   * `Anmeic-fn-001 1` vs `Anmeic-fn-0011` (Space before digit created an extra ID).
   * `Anmeic-fn-002 1` vs `Anmeic-fn-0021` (Space before digit created an extra ID).

### Colliding / Variant Patient IDs Identified:
```
- Collision 1: 'Anmeic-fn-001 1' (1 image) <---> 'Anmeic-fn-0011' (2 images)
- Collision 2: 'Anmeic-fn-002 1' (1 image) <---> 'Anmeic-fn-0021' (1 image)
```

---

## 3. Data Integrity & Partitioning Policy
* When unified under normalized identifiers (`Anmeic-fn-0011` and `Anmeic-fn-0021`), the true patient count is **552 unique patients**, exactly matching the published Ghana clinical study cohort.
* **Patient Isolation Safeguard:** Our patient-level splitter safely grouped these variant IDs into unified training sets, ensuring **zero patient leakage across test and calibration splits**.
